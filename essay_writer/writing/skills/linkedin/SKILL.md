# LinkedIn Post Writing

## Purpose

Write a professional social post with a clear point, credible detail, and an appropriate invitation to respond.

## Required inputs

Determine the speaker, audience, news or insight, desired perception, supporting facts, and whether a link or call to action is required.

## Hard constraints

- Never invent traction, customer reactions, revenue, team size, quotations, or launch status.
- Avoid engagement bait, fake vulnerability, and unsupported market claims.
- Use no more than three relevant hashtags unless the user requests another convention.

## Soft guidance

Lead with the concrete news or observation. Use readable paragraph breaks without turning every sentence into a one-line paragraph. Prefer a specific closing question over a generic request for thoughts.

## Research and citations

Links may appear naturally when useful. Always retain researched sources in metadata.

## Pre-delivery checklist

Verify every metric and status claim, speaker voice, platform readability, link accuracy, hashtag restraint, and call to action.
