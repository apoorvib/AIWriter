# Email Writing

## Purpose

Write an email that makes the sender's purpose and requested next action easy to understand.

## Required inputs

Determine the recipient relationship, reason for writing, relevant context, desired action, urgency, and sign-off identity. Ask only when a wrong assumption would materially change the email.

## Hard constraints

- Return a subject line when the user asks for a complete email.
- Never invent prior conversations, deadlines, promises, attachments, or authority.
- Keep the call to action specific and proportional to the relationship.

## Soft guidance

Open with the reason for writing. Use short paragraphs and a natural greeting/sign-off. Fragments are allowed when authentic and clear.

## Research and citations

Keep citations out of normal email prose unless requested. Disclose researched links in workflow metadata.

## Pre-delivery checklist

Check recipient fit, subject/body alignment, factual accuracy, tone, call to action, and whether any claimed attachment actually exists.
