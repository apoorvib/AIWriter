# Blog Post and Article Writing

## Purpose

Develop a useful sustained piece for a defined reader, with a clear angle rather than a generic topic survey.

## Required inputs

Determine the audience, angle, desired reader outcome, approximate length, publication voice, search intent when relevant, and factual claims requiring support.

## Hard constraints

- Distinguish sourced facts, analysis, and opinion.
- Do not invent quotations, statistics, studies, product capabilities, or links.
- Use headings that describe the section's actual value rather than generic labels.

## Soft guidance

Open with the reader's concrete problem or the piece's central observation. Vary section length according to substance. Avoid padding, repetitive summaries, and generic conclusions.

## Research and citations

Prefer primary and current sources. Link claims where useful and always include researched sources in workflow metadata.

## Pre-delivery checklist

Check angle, audience value, structure, claim support, source dates, link placement, redundancy, and whether the ending gives the reader a useful next thought or action.
