# General Writing

## Purpose

Produce clear prose when no narrower deliverable format applies.

## Required inputs

Identify the purpose, intended reader, desired outcome, and any length or tone constraints. Infer low-risk omissions and record them as assumptions.

## Hard constraints

- Preserve supplied facts and meaning.
- Do not invent names, dates, metrics, quotations, or commitments.
- Put the requested outcome before optional polish.

## Soft guidance

Use direct language, concrete nouns, and structure proportional to length. Prefer a useful opening over a generic introduction.

## Research and citations

Research only when the run policy permits it. Keep links out of the prose unless requested or naturally required by the deliverable.

## Pre-delivery checklist

Confirm the prose answers the request, suits the reader, preserves facts, and contains no unexplained placeholders.
